Professional summary

Software engineer turning AI ideas into systems you can measure.

I've spent 3+ years as a software engineer at Crusade Technologies, Sanizer and NTT Data, and I'm now completing an MS in Computer Science at George Washington University.

My focus is the systems side of AI: retrieval-augmented generation, MCP servers that give assistants real tools, cost-aware inference, and the data pipelines and serving layers underneath. PaperMind

An MCP server that gives Claude a searchable, citable library of research papers. Hybrid search fuses pgvector with Postgres full-text using Reciprocal Rank Fusion, tuned on a labeled 36-question eval. Bearer-token auth, a multi-stage Docker image, and two production bugs found and fixed through testing.

Computer vision · Vision-LLM · v1.0.0

SentryEye

Traffic-incident detection from CCTV with a cheap-to-expensive cascade: YOLOv11 and ByteTrack watch every frame, motion signals flag anomalies, and a vision-LLM writes a typed incident report only when something looks wrong. Evaluated on the TAD benchmark of real accident footage.

~0.1%of frames reach the vision-LLM
